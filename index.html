Questions people ask.

Is the quote actually flat?

Yes. We price the job upfront and confirm it on-site before we start. The only thing that changes the number is if there's noticeably more to it than described.

Do I need to be home?

Not if we can access the area from outside — a driveway, path, deck or roof. Just let us know when you book and we'll send a photo once it's done.

How fast can you come?

Message before 5pm and there's a good chance we can get to it tomorrow. Quotes go out the same day you ask.

Will it damage my surfaces?

No — we dial the pressure and method to suit what we're cleaning, using a gentler soft-wash approach on painted surfaces, render, roofs and timber.

Do you supply the water?

We can generally use an outdoor tap on-site. If there's no water access, let us know when you book and we'll sort it.
